Inventory

Fuse & Bond §4.5

A tool at 0 durability is Broken — SLP repair won't touch it at any price. The only way back is Fusion: burn a same-rarity tool as fuel for a deterministic partial restore. Epic/Mystic also bond a small AXS toll into non-transferable bAXS on top of the SLP fee, to keep AXS scarce.

Codex

Blind Boxes

Uses a local commit–reveal demo (§7): a seed is hashed and shown before you open, then revealed after so the roll is checkable — same shape as the on-chain flow, no chain required for this prototype.

Refinery §5.1

Smelted from ore, not bought — same odds shape as Blind Boxes, just paid in ore. Owning one boosts every shift's yield (best Refinery only, doesn't stack). It has its own durability, repairable in SLP or ore, no roll — it never breaks, just goes idle.

Owned

Marketplace

Direct purchase (§7) — buy the exact tool you want at a fixed USDC price, no gambling. Priced above blind-box expected value on purpose: boxes must always win on price, this is the certainty option.
