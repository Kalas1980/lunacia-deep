Lunacia Deep

An idle mining game on Ronin where your Axies' tools can genuinely break — and getting that wrong costs you a second tool, not just SLP.

Built with Claude · Working prototype, not a mockup

The Problem

Most idle mining games have one button: wait.

Durability in the genre is usually decoration — a bar that ticks down and gets topped back up with a tap. There's no decision in it, no risk, nothing to actually manage.

Lunacia Deep makes durability the whole game.

Axies are labor, tools are capital. Every class has a mining role (§3), and every tool tier has a real reason to be repaired, not replaced.

The Mechanic That Makes It a Game

Zero durability isn't an inconvenience. It's a wall.

SLP repair works between 1 and 100 durability — success chance falls the emptier you run it. Let it hit exactly 0 and the tool goes BROKEN. SLP can't save it at any price.

The only way back: Fusion Repair — burn a second same-rarity tool as fuel for a deterministic partial restore. Every player, at every tier, has to actually watch this number.

The Upgrade Path

Burn two tools. Maybe get a better one.

Reforge two same-rarity tools into one. Odds scale continuously with the condition of what you feed in — fresh tools upgrade more often, worn ones mostly just match or flaw. Common pairs can genuinely tier up into Rare.
